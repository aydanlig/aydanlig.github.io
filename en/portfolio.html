Drawn by feel

Expressive portraits in fineliner, ink and watercolour – live on the spot, on stage or from a photo.

Artist statement · draft

I draw people by feel – not the way they look in a photo, but the way they feel in that moment. With fineliner, ink and watercolour I work fast and direct: lines may smear, colour may bleed, chance is part of it.

My subjects are people, Berlin and the short live moments in between – at Mauerpark, at concerts, at festivals. Every portrait happens on the spot, in a few minutes, face to face.
