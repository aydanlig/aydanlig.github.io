For long-distance relationships & gifts: Getting drawn so your favourite person far away has a piece of you? Then you keep the original – and a canvas or framed print of it goes straight to their home, even abroad. It's printed in the recipient's country, so it doesn't travel far. Just tick "Also as a canvas / print" when you order and you get a separate quote for it.

Live at your place or bigger ideas (e.g. a large family picture)? I'll also come to your home, your office or your favourite spot and draw you there – surcharge by arrangement. Best to write to me directly by e-mail.

Discount card, shipping & terms

Discount card: Did you get a card from me in person? Then you get €5 off every portrait (one card per order, no cash payout). For an event booking or the Kleckerkasten workshop the card even counts as €50 off!

Shipping within Germany: €4.90, with frame €6.90 (safely packed). Pick-up in Berlin is free. Shipping abroad on request.

No revisions: Every portrait is a hand-painted one-off. Changes would mean a completely new picture and are therefore not possible once it's finished.

No right of withdrawal: Commissioned portraits are made individually to your wishes. For such custom-made items there is no statutory right of withdrawal (§ 312g para. 2 no. 1 BGB, German Civil Code). Your statutory warranty rights remain unaffected.
