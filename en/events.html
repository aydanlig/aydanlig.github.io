B · The Kleckerkasten

Expressive team building: just splash away

The Kleckerkasten (German for “splash box”) is a mobile creative workshop inspired by methods of art therapy: a creative counterbalance to everyday work. I bring canvases, paints and painting supplies straight to you. Your team experiments freely – no pressure to perform, no “right” or “wrong”. The result is a large shared artwork that gets a place in your offices.

On request: individual live portraits of your staff as a personal keepsake to take home.

  • Stress relief: head off, hands on. Painting noticeably slows things down.
  • Team spirit: colleagues see a new side of each other.
  • No pressure: no experience, no judging, no talent needed.
  • A lasting result: a shared canvas for your rooms.
01
