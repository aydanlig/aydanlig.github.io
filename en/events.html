Frequently asked questions

How much space do you need?

About 2–4 m² for a folding table (1.35 m) and seats for the guests. Indoors or outdoors, no power needed.

How many guests can you draw?

Up to 12 quick portraits per hour or 2–6 detailed ones with watercolour. For lots of guests I recommend quick portraits or a mix.

Do you travel outside Berlin?

Yes, for a surcharge: outside Berlin I add 25% to the total price to cover travel costs.

Can guests pay for themselves?

Yes: a flat fee from you, guests pay on site, or donation-based – you can choose in the form.

B · Team building workshop

The Klecker­kasten!

Expressive team building: just splash away

The Kleckerkasten (German for “splash box”) is a mobile creative workshop inspired by methods of art therapy: a creative counterbalance to everyday work. I bring canvases, paints and painting supplies straight to you. Your team experiments freely – no pressure to perform, no “right” or “wrong”. The result is a large shared artwork that gets a place in your offices.

On request: individual live portraits of your staff as a personal keepsake to take home.

  • Stress relief: head off, hands on. Painting noticeably slows things down.
  • Team spirit: colleagues see a new side of each other.
  • No pressure: no experience, no judging, no talent needed.
  • A lasting result: a shared canvas for your rooms.
01

Arrive

Short warm-up exercises with paint to let go of the “I can't paint”.

02

Express

Everyone paints intuitively. Then the pictures move on and get painted further together.

03

Connect

Everything flows into one big team canvas. A short reflection: what do we take away?
