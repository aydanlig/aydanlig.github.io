Who am I?

“I draw from the gut – and look for the picture that fits each person exactly.”

I'm Aydan Lig'on, born in 2005, at home in Berlin and raised bilingual – with roots in Germany and the USA. At school, art and English were my advanced subjects, and my portraits have already hung in Berlin restaurants.

When I draw, I like to ask about the style: ink and fineliner feel serious and clear, watercolour playful and a little chaotic – both together show a many-layered character.

Right now, art gets all my time: drawing outdoors, events and the Kleckerkasten. By September 2027 it should be able to support me on its own. If it doesn't, I'll switch to a work-study degree and keep drawing part-time afterwards.

  • My 3 F's: family, fitness and freedom.
  • What fascinates me: digital art, videography, game design, psychology and entrepreneurship.
  • Drawing with others: at the Castle Drawing Meetup in Berlin.

Why I do this

Sometimes I draw someone completely spontaneously – and days later I get a message like this. A small portrait can make a hard day a little lighter. That's exactly why I do this.

My goal

What I want to be world-class at

  1. Lightning-fast watercolour portraits that blow you away. Watercolour is a slow medium – I bend it with speed technique and ink into fast, stunning handmade art no AI could ever copy.
  2. Bringing people together. Connecting teams and Berlin neighbourhoods through live portraits and art workshops.
  3. Art for everyone. Handmade art that anyone in Berlin can afford – so we appreciate art, our friendships, our time in this world and local craftsmanship a little more.
  4. The most fun workshops in town. In the Kleckerkasten, grown-ups get to be kids again: splashing paint, laughing and expressing themselves with friends or colleagues.
What I believe

What I stand for – and against

I stand for

  • Handmade art. Real ink, real watercolour, real paper: every picture one of a kind, drawn live in front of you.
  • Honesty. Clear prices and open words – even when something goes wrong.
  • Community through art. A portrait in the park or at a gig gets strangers talking. That's what Berlin means to me.
  • Using AI wisely. AI helps me with emails and planning – so there's more time for drawing.

I'm against

  • Art that doesn't get enough support. Many Berlin artists are underpaid. The district officially doesn't let me draw and sell in Mauerpark – I'd love more room for street art.
  • AI "art" that rips off artists. Stirred together without consent, credit or pay. With me it's only the real thing: by hand, right in front of you.

Questions and answers

What do you see differently from others in your industry?

Many people say that since Covid hardly anyone buys art, and nobody wants real experiences any more. I disagree. Right now, with AI spitting out images in seconds, handmade art is worth more than ever: a real moment, captured by a person – in a picture someone drew just for you.

On Halloween a woman walked past my portrait stand and said to her friend, "Come on, let's get drawn. We'll remember this forever – and later the picture will make us so happy." Her friend said "Next time", but she kept at it. I drew them both, and they were happy. That's exactly why I believe in real art.

May I use or remix your work?

For private, non-commercial purposes you're welcome to share, use and remix my work – make something paw-some out of it! Please credit me (SmileyFoxyPortraits).

Portraits of people are the exception: the people in them have the right to their own image. For commercial use, just drop me a line at aydan@smileyfoxyportraits.com.

Can I hire you, interview you or do a project with you?

Absolutely! Whether it's an event, interview, talk, collaboration, project idea or a correction – write to me at aydan@smileyfoxyportraits.com. For events and team workshops you can also go straight to the events page.

Which pronouns do you use?

I'm genderfluid and use all pronouns – mostly he/him, but also she/her and they/them.

Let's talk!

Whether it's a portrait idea, an event, a collaboration or just a “Hi, I saw you at Mauerpark!” – I'm genuinely happy about every message and read everything myself. Write to me at aydan@smileyfoxyportraits.com or visit the contact page.

Removal requests: you're in a picture on my website or on Instagram and don't want to be anymore? Write to me at the same address and I'll take it down.
