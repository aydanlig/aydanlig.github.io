Want to be sure? Whether and where exactly I'm drawing is posted beforehand in my Instagram story.

Mauerpark

  • When: Saturday 2–10 pm · Sunday often from 11 am
  • Exactly where: Bernauer Str. 53, right next to the photo booth – Google Maps ↗
  • Getting there: U Eberswalder Straße or S+U Gesundbrunnen

Between the flea market, karaoke and street music, this is where most of my €1 portraits are made – art that really anyone can afford.

Brandenburg Gate

  • When: now and then, whenever I feel like it – rarely outside tourist season
  • Getting there: S+U Brandenburger Tor
