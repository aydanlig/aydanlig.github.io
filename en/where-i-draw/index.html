Where & when I draw

Live portraits: A6/A7 €5, A5 from €15, A4 from €28. Saturdays only at Mauerpark: the A6/A7 quick sketch for €1 – art for everyone. In good weather; pay in cash, by card or with PayPal.

Upcoming dates

Markets, Christmas markets and bands I draw live – past dates disappear by themselves.

Current dates are in my Instagram story.

Mauerpark

  • When: every Saturday 2–10 pm
  • Exactly where: Bernauer Str. 53, right next to the photo booth – Google Maps ↗
  • Getting there: U Eberswalder Straße or S+U Gesundbrunnen

Between the flea market, karaoke and street music, this is where my €1 quick sketch happens on Saturdays – art that really anyone can afford.

Organic market at Kollwitzplatz

  • When: on selected Thursdays, approx. 12–7 pm (Jan–Mar until 6 pm) – dates
  • Exactly where: Kollwitzplatz, Prenzlauer Berg – Google Maps ↗
  • Getting there: U Senefelderplatz

Christmas markets

  • Charlottenburg Palace: in the pagoda tent, on selected evenings from 5 pm – dates
  • ZK/U Gütermarkt: winter Christmas edition on Sun 13 Dec, Siemensstr. 27 (Moabit)

A portrait as a Christmas present, drawn live while you sip your mulled wine.

Gigs at Schokoladen

  • When: on gig nights – which bands is listed under dates
  • Exactly where: Schokoladen, Ackerstr. 169, Berlin-Mitte – Google Maps ↗

During the set I draw the band live. After the show I'm happy to draw you too – just come and say hi. Bands I've drawn so far →

Brandenburg Gate

  • When: now and then, whenever I feel like it – rarely outside tourist season
  • Getting there: S+U Brandenburger Tor

Rainy days: I'm looking for a place

When it rains, I can't draw outdoors. Do you have a café, restaurant or room where I could offer portraits on rainy days? I'd love to hear from you.
