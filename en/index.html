Why I draw like this

Art is my bridge to people.

I draw from the gut, not with a ruler – and look for the picture that fits each person exactly.

My portraits happen live, in a few minutes, right in front of you: with fineliner and ink, which feel clear and serious, and with watercolour, which is playful and a little chaotic. Whatever goes wrong stays in – that's exactly what makes every picture a one-off. I draw strangers on Berlin's trains, musicians on stage, teams at company parties and people who sit down at Mauerpark for one euro. What I'm after is the moment someone recognises themselves and laughs.

Ink & finelineroften feels serious, clear and calm
Watercolourplayful, cheerful and a little chaotic
Both mixedmany-layered – strict and open at the same time

– Aydan
