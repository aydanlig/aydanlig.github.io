      About me · My mission

      Hi, I'm Aydan Lig'on – and art is my bridge to people.

      I’ve always had this unstoppable urge to create something. It started at the drawing meetups of the art group “Creative Session”, my creative family back then – and then on Berlin’s trains: I drew strangers and simply gave them the portraits.

      The smiles, the reactions, the encouragement – that’s how a passion became a business.

      “I draw by feeling – and look for the picture that fits each person exactly.”

      That’s why I ask about the style first – every material tells something different about you:

      Ink & finelineroften feels serious, clear and calm Watercolourplayful, cheerful and a little chaotic Both mixedlayered – strictness and openness at once
      01

      Why I started

      Because a five-minute drawing can make two strangers laugh. Art has always been my quickest way to a real connection.

      02

      Why I keep going

      To show that creative independence beyond soulless office routines is possible. I’ve given myself one year fully for this work – and I’m giving it 100 %.

      03

      My mission

      Creating real local community and deeper human connections – through fast, expressive live art.

      How I see it differently: real art is not out

      Many say that since Covid hardly anyone buys art, and nobody wants handmade, real experiences any more. I see it differently. People still love the real thing – capturing a moment with a picture someone drew just for them. Especially in times of AI, when you often can’t tell what’s generated and what’s truly handmade and good, real handmade art is worth more than ever.

      “Come on, let’s get drawn. We’ll remember this forever – and later the picture will make us so happy.”

      On Halloween a woman walked past my portrait stand and stopped. Her friend said: “Next time.” But she didn’t let go – with exactly that sentence. So I drew the two of them, and they walked on happy. For me that’s the proof: the need for real, handmade art hasn’t gone away. Sometimes it just takes a little nudge.

      I stand for honesty, community through art and AI used wisely – and against AI “art” that copies artists. What I stand for – and against →
