Art auctions

I auction selected originals here. You bid with your first name and one way to reach you (email, phone or delivery address) – only your first name is shown. Whoever is ahead at the end gets the picture.

Loading auctions …

Auction rules

  • Binding: every bid is a binding offer to buy. The highest bid when the auction ends makes the purchase.
  • No starting price: you decide what the picture is worth to you. Each new bid only has to be higher than the current one.
  • No last-second sniping: if a bid comes in during the last 2 minutes, the auction is extended by 2 minutes.
  • After the end: within 48 hours I contact the highest bidder (by email, phone or in person at the delivery address) with a payment link (PayPal or card). Payment within 7 days.
  • Shipping: within Germany €4.90 (with frame €6.90), pick-up in Berlin free, abroad on request.
  • Right of withdrawal: as a private person you have the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal (German).
  • Privacy: your contact details (email, phone, address) stay private, are only used to handle the sale and are deleted afterwards. No VAT is charged under § 19 UStG (small business).

I announce new auctions on my email list.
